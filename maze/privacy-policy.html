Maze : Clean & Simple Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026 · Operator: OnePixel

OnePixel ("the Operator") respects your privacy while providing the mobile game Maze : Clean & Simple ("the Game"). The Game has no account system and the Operator does not run its own backend server or store user data on any server. This document explains what information is actually processed by the Game and how.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not require sign-up, login, or a nickname. The Operator does not directly collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The Operator never receives or stores your payment information (such as card numbers). The Game does not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.

2. Information Stored Only on Your Device

The following information is stored only locally on your device and is never transmitted to the Operator's servers. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its app data.

  • Mazes in progress for each difficulty (the maze, the path you have drawn, your undo history, and the elapsed time)
  • Your top five times and the number of mazes solved for each difficulty, with the date of each record (times with and without hints are kept separately), and the number of hints used in each maze in progress
  • Selected theme and difficulty, gameplay settings (such as timer, animations, and sound effects) and language settings
  • Which in-app products ("Remove Ads", Theme Pack) have been purchased on this device
  • An internal counter used only to pace how often ads are shown (the number of mazes finished and the time the last ad was shown - not personally identifiable)

3. Information Automatically Collected (Third-Party Services)

The Game uses services provided by Google. The following information may be automatically collected by Google, not by the Operator.

If you buy "Remove Ads", the Game no longer starts the ad service, so this information is no longer collected through ads.

3-2. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

When you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details and purchase history. The Operator does not receive your payment information.

4. Ads and How to Opt Out of Personalized Ads

The Game shows only one kind of ad: an interstitial ad (a full-screen ad), shown occasionally right after you finish a maze and close the record window. Ads are never shown while you are solving a maze, when the app starts, when you start a new game, when you view your records, or when you return to the app. No ad is shown after your first finished maze, and ads are shown at most once every 8 minutes.

The Game does not show banner ads, rewarded ads, or app-open ads. If you buy "Remove Ads", no ads are shown at all.

If you do not want personalized ads, you can opt out from your device settings.

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

The Game is distributed worldwide. For users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the United Kingdom, Google's UMP (User Messaging Platform) shows a consent form asking whether you consent to the use of data for ads (as required by GDPR). You can review and change your choice at any time from in-game Settings → Ad privacy options (this option is only shown to users in those regions).

5. In-App Purchase

The Game offers the following in-app products, each a one-time purchase:

  • Remove Ads: removes all ads from the Game
  • Theme Pack: adds extra color themes (the four basic themes are free)

Gameplay features are never sold. Purchases are processed by Google Play. The Game stores on your device only which items were purchased, and when the app starts it checks your Google Play purchase history to restore purchases automatically if you reinstall the app or change devices. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

6. Update Check

When the app starts, it downloads a small public file from the Operator's website to check whether a newer version of the Game is required. This request does not include any personal information, and the Operator does not keep logs of it.

8. International Data Transfer

The Game may be distributed worldwide, and information processed by Google (AdMob, Google Play) may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own (including the United States, where Google's servers are located).

9. Retention and Deletion

Because the Operator does not run its own server, no user data is retained by the Operator. Data stored on your device (mazes in progress, records, settings) is deleted immediately when you uninstall the app or clear its app data. Retention of data collected by Google (such as the advertising ID) follows Google's own policies and settings.

10. Your Rights

Since the Operator does not store any personal data on a server, there is no Operator-held data to access, correct, or delete. You can still exercise related rights as follows (including users in the EEA and the United Kingdom under the GDPR):

  • Delete data stored on your device: uninstall the app or clear its app data. Note that this also deletes your mazes in progress and records; purchases are restored from Google Play.
  • Reset/delete your advertising ID: Device Settings → Google → Ads
  • Change your ad consent choice (EEA/UK): in-game Settings → Ad privacy options
  • Other inquiries, or requests to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of data: contact the email address under "Contact" below

11. Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents have the right under the CCPA/CPRA to opt out of the sale or sharing of their personal information. The Operator does not directly sell personal information; advertising-related data processing is handled through Google AdMob. Please contact the email address below for related requests.

12. Children's Privacy

The Game is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at the email below so we can review and take appropriate action.

13. Security Measures

Because the Operator does not run its own server and does not store or manage user personal information separately, the structural risk of a server-side data breach is low. The app follows platform-level (Google Play) security measures such as code signing.

14. Breach Notification

Since the Operator manages no personal data directly, the likelihood of a breach is low. If an incident affecting users is identified through a third-party service (such as Google AdMob), we will notify affected users within a reasonable time via this page or an in-app notice, once we become aware of it.

미로 : 깔끔하고 심플하게 (Maze : Clean & Simple) 개인정보처리방침

시행일: 2026년 10월 30일 · 운영자: OnePixel

OnePixel(이하 "운영자")은 모바일 게임 미로 : 깔끔하고 심플하게 (Maze : Clean & Simple)(이하 "본 게임")를 제공하면서 이용자의 개인정보를 소중히 다룹니다. 본 게임은 회원가입이 없고 운영자가 자체 서버를 두지 않으며, 이용자 데이터를 서버에 보관하지 않습니다. 이 문서는 본 게임에서 실제로 처리되는 정보와 그 방식을 설명합니다.

1. 수집하지 않는 정보

본 게임은 회원가입, 로그인, 닉네임 등록을 요구하지 않습니다. 이름, 이메일, 전화번호, 정확한 위치정보 등 이용자를 직접 식별할 수 있는 정보를 운영자가 직접 수집하지 않습니다. 카드 번호 등 결제 정보는 운영자가 받거나 저장하지 않습니다. 본 게임은 이용 분석이나 오류 수집 서비스를 사용하지 않습니다.

2. 기기에만 저장되는 정보

아래 정보는 이용자의 기기 안(로컬 저장소)에만 저장되며, 운영자의 서버로 전송되지 않습니다. 앱을 삭제하거나 앱 데이터를 초기화하면 함께 삭제됩니다.

  • 난이도별로 풀고 있는 미로(미로 모양, 지나온 길, 되돌리기 기록, 경과 시간)
  • 난이도별 상위 5개 기록과 완료 횟수, 각 기록의 날짜(힌트를 쓴 기록과 쓰지 않은 기록은 따로 저장), 풀고 있는 미로에서 쓴 힌트 수
  • 선택한 테마와 난이도, 게임 설정(시간 표시, 애니메이션, 효과음 등), 언어 설정
  • 이 기기에서 구매한 인앱 상품("광고 제거", 테마 팩)
  • 광고 노출 빈도 조절용 내부 카운터(끝낸 미로 수와 마지막 광고 시각 — 개인을 식별할 수 있는 정보 아님)

4. 광고 및 맞춤 광고 거부 방법

본 게임의 광고는 전면 광고(전체 화면 광고) 한 종류뿐이며, 미로를 끝내고 기록 창을 닫은 직후에 가끔 노출됩니다. 미로를 푸는 도중, 앱을 켤 때, 새 게임을 시작할 때, 기록을 볼 때, 앱으로 돌아올 때에는 광고가 노출되지 않습니다. 처음 끝낸 미로 뒤에는 광고가 나오지 않으며, 광고는 최대 8분에 한 번만 노출됩니다.

본 게임에는 배너 광고, 보상형 광고, 앱 오프닝 광고가 없습니다. "광고 제거"를 구매하면 광고가 전혀 노출되지 않습니다.

맞춤형 광고를 원하지 않으시면 기기 설정에서 거부할 수 있습니다.

  • Android: 설정 → Google → 광고 → "광고 개인 최적화 선택 해제"
